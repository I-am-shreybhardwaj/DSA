## Learning Roadmap

1. Start with **medium Stack patterns** if not already mastered.
2. Focus on **complex monotonic stack optimizations**.
3. Attempt **advanced expression evaluation and parsing**.
4. Tackle **complex bracket matching and validation**.
5. Master **histogram and skyline problems**.
6. Solve **advanced min/max with DP**.
7. Learn **advanced stack design patterns** (LRU, LFU).
8. Explore **complex recursive patterns with stack**.
9. Study **skyline and 2D histogram problems**.
10. Consolidate with **hard classic problems** for interviews and competitive programming.

---

## Advanced Techniques

- **Monotonic Stack**: Essential for range queries, histogram, and rectangle problems.
- **Stack vs Recursion**: Often more efficient and avoids stack overflow.
- **Expression Parsing**: Use precedence and associativity rules carefully.
- **LRU/LFU Implementation**: Critical for system design interviews.
- **DP with Stack**: Combine for complex optimization problems.
- **Skyline Algorithm**: Multi-level processing with stack.
- **2D Histogram**: Apply histogram approach to 2D problems.

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Stack** is fundamental for solving many categories of hard problems.
- **Monotonic patterns** are powerful but require careful implementation.
- **Design patterns** (LRU, LFU) are essential for interview preparation.
- **Expression evaluation** requires understanding operator precedence.
- **Histogram problems**: Core pattern for many hard-level problems.
- Practice both **iterative and recursive** approaches where applicable.
- Always consider **space and time complexity** trade-offs.
