## Learning Roadmap

1. Strengthen your **easy LinkedList patterns** if not mastered.
2. Start with **medium two pointer & fast/slow pointer problems**.
3. Progress to **reversing with constraints** and **complex deletions**.
4. Explore **merging and sorting LinkedLists**.
5. Attempt **cycle detection and palindrome operations**.
6. Practice **stack & queue integration** patterns.
7. Learn **DP on LinkedLists**.
8. Consolidate with **medium-level classic problems**.
9. Focus on **copy with random pointer** and **complex reordering**.

---

## Key Insights

- Always use **dummy nodes** for cleaner code.
- Master **fast and slow pointer** for finding middle.
- Understand **Floyd's cycle detection** algorithm deeply.
- Practice **in-place modifications** to optimize space.
- Reverse operations are crucial; practice various reverse patterns.
- Use **recursion carefully** due to stack space constraints.
- **Sorting LinkedLists**: Use merge sort (O(n log n) time, O(log n) space).
