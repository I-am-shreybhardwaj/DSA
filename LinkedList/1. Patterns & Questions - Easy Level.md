## Learning Roadmap

1. Start with **traversal and basic operations** to understand node structure.
2. Practice **two pointers and fast/slow pointer** techniques.
3. Master **reversing linkedlists** (both iterative and recursive).
4. Learn **cycle detection** using Floyd's algorithm.
5. Practice **deletion, modification, and merging** operations.
6. Solve **palindrome and duplicate removal** problems.
7. Explore **stack & queue integration** with LinkedLists.
8. Build confidence with **miscellaneous classic problems**.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Always handle **edge cases** (null, single node, two nodes).
- Use **dummy nodes** to simplify operations on the head.
- Leverage **two pointers** for efficient traversal.
- Remember **Floyd's cycle detection** for cycle problems.
- Practice **in-place modifications** to reduce space complexity.
- **Reverse operations** are fundamental; practice various patterns.
- Use **recursion carefully** - consider iterative solutions first.
